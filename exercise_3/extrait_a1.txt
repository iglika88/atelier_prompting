This is the story of Rob in Sherwood Forest wi They wear green clothes
King Richard is a good king but he is on a crusade. He is not in England. So his brother, Prince John, acts as king.
Robin Hood and his merry men like King Richard. They don't like Prince John. Robin Hood takes money from the rich and gives it to the poor.
Prince John and the Sheriff of Nottingham hate Robin Hood. But Robin has friends, his merry men, Little John and Friar Juck.
Robin has another friend, Maid Marian. Robin is in love with Maid Marian and she is in love with Robin.
Fortunately, King Richard returns to England and is king again. Robin marries Marian.

(source: The Legend of King Arthur and the Legend of Robin Hood, Harrap's School)
